## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Captaline, and what problem does it solve?

Captaline is a premium Eleventy (11ty) JAMSTACK theme built for company profile, business, startup landing, and portfolio websites. It ships with 14+ sections and widget areas, five distinct service page designs, product and service lists with detail pages, blog list and post pages, tag list and tag detail pages, static pages, a contact form forwarded to email, and a media manager. Content is managed through Tina CMS, and SEO meta tags, Twitter Cards, and Open Graph tags are generated automatically from each page's title and description. Instead of building a company profile site, its sections, and its CMS from scratch, you install the source, edit content through the admin panel, and the site is live.

### Do I need to know Eleventy or Node.js to use Captaline?

Basic Node.js knowledge helps for the initial install, but daily content editing happens through Tina CMS in the browser. Install Node.js from nodejs.org, download the source code, extract it into a project folder, then run `npm install && npm start`. The frontend runs at `localhost:8080` and the CMS admin at `localhost:8080/admin`. After that, you edit configuration, homepage, about, services, blog, static pages, and media from the admin panel without touching code.

### What does "programmatic project" mean in Captaline?

It means you do not hardcode design or content changes. Sections, products, services, blog posts, tags, and static pages are all driven by the content management system, so adding or updating content happens in the CMS and flows into the templates. This is why the theme ships with 14+ sections and widget areas that can be shown or hidden per area without editing layout files.

### What are the five service designs, and how do I use them?

Captaline includes Service 1 through Service 5, each with its own design for product and service pages. In the CMS, open the Service 1 through Service 5 menus and create a new article by pressing the plus button. Pick the design that fits each service by placing the article in the corresponding service menu. The service list page then displays all services, and the detail page renders with the design you chose.

### How do I connect Tina Cloud to the project?

Register at Tina Cloud with your GitHub account, create a new project, select your repository, and insert your domain URL. Copy the Client ID and Token. In your project, open `tina/config.ts`, then paste the Client ID into `clientId` and the Token into `token`. Save the file. With Tina Cloud connected, you can develop the backend online and edit content from the cloud dashboard.

### Is SEO handled automatically?

Yes. Captaline injects an auto SEO script that follows each page's title and description to generate meta tags, Twitter Cards, and Open Graph tags. Dynamic pages for the product and service areas and the blog, plus dedicated tag list and tag detail pages, are wired for SEO. Combined with Eleventy's static output and the fast, responsive design, the theme is built for search-ready pages without manual meta wiring per page.

### How do I deploy Captaline?

Push the project to GitHub first: create a repository, then run the `git init`, `git add`, `git commit`, `git remote add origin`, and `git push` commands from the article, replacing the username, repo name, and branch with yours. Then create an account with your GitHub account on Netlify or Vercel, create a new project, connect your repository, select Eleventy as the framework, and click deploy. Netlify and Vercel are the recommended hosts for an Eleventy site.

### What pages and features are included?

Static pages for contact, about, privacy policy, and other content; dynamic pages for products, services, and blog; tag list and tag detail pages; product and service list pages with detailed pages in five design themes; blog list and post article pages; mobile and desktop image loaders; widget areas that can be shown or hidden per area; a contact form forwarded to your email; a media manager for PDFs and images; and project documentation with help and support.

### Can I use Captaline for client projects, and what is the license?

Yes. The theme is a one-time purchase and you own 100% of the source code, so you can use it on personal and client projects without recurring fees. The license does not permit reselling or redistributing the theme itself as a competing product. If you need a fully custom build, custom work is available through the Fiverr link in the theme listing.

### Where can I see the demo and get support?

The live demo is at `captaline.pages.dev`. The article also includes a video walkthrough. For support, email creativebydre@gmail.com. Custom development and AI automation work are available through the Fiverr link in the theme listing.
